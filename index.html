Method & sources

This is a historical-critical comparison tool: 27 ancient documents, not one flattened text. Quantitative dimensions are measurements or broad scholarly ranges. Interpretive dimensions are comparative editorial models on a 0–100 scale.

Date ranges are intentionally broad where dating is contested. Greek length is approximate and varies slightly by critical edition. Interpretive scores are arguments made visible; they are not measurements and should be tested against the texts.

The interface shows uncertainty bars only for date ranges. It does not invent statistical error bars for interpretive judgments.
